About

ByteFest is GEMS New Millennium School's annual tech festival — a day where coders, robot-builders, AI tinkerers and gamers take over the campus. It started as a hackathon and grew into six tracks across four arenas.

Whether you ship code in your sleep or you've never written a line, there's a track built for you. Bring an idea, a team, or just curiosity.

FAQ

What is ByteFest?

A one-day tech festival hosted by GEMS New Millennium School, Dubai Hills. Six tracks spanning competitive coding, robotics, AI and e-sports, all under one roof.

Who can take part?

ByteFest is for school students. Each track has its own age group and team rules — open a track's full brief on the wheel above to see its grade and capacity.

What if I can't code yet?

Come anyway. GameGrid and the robotics tracks reward very different skills from coding, and every track is a chance to learn something.

Can I enter more than one track?

Some tracks run at the same time, so check the schedule once it's published. The organising team will confirm which combinations are possible.

How do I register?

Registration details will be posted here and sent to participating schools. Until then, reach the team at bytefestgnms@gmail.com.

Who do I contact with questions?

Email bytefestgnms@gmail.com — the core team reads every message.
